Still have questions?

Do I need to be there for viewings?

No. Tenants book from your photos, video tour and floor plan, and ask questions in the chat. There are no physical viewings on HousingAnywhere, which protects both you and the tenant.

When and how do I get paid?

The tenant pays when they book, and the money is held securely. It's released to you 48 hours after they move in, with the commission deducted from that first payout.

What if the tenant cancels?

Once the 24-hour cancellation window after booking has passed, the first month's rent is guaranteed to you, and your calendar opens up again for new bookings.

How do I hand over the keys if I'm away?

Agree on a handover in the chat, for example through a trusted contact or a key box. Keeping that agreement in the chat means it's on record for both of you.

Is HousingAnywhere an agency?

No. HousingAnywhere is a platform that connects you with tenants and handles the booking and payments. The rental agreement is between you and your tenant.

Which rules do I need to follow in my country?

That depends on where your property is. The assistant in the corner of this page can walk you through the basics for your country, and you can check the sample rental contracts before you publish.

Design note: localised answersThe current private-landlord FAQ shows Italian legal requirements on other countries' pages. Country-specific answers would come from the assistant, drafted with AI and reviewed by a person before going live.
